How I work

See everything first.

A recommendation is worth little until every account, entity, and commitment is on one page. The balance sheet comes before any opinion.

Count every fee.

Few families know what they pay in total. The assessment finds out, including the fees buried inside funds and products.

Answer the question asked.

The family wants to know whether to build a family office. The report recommends the structure that fits, explains why, and shows what each option would cost.

Disclose the conflict.

If the answer is yes, I could be a candidate to run the office or serve as its Chief Investment Officer. I say so at the first meeting, and my fee is the same whatever the verdict.
