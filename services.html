Services

Every engagement starts with the assessment. It gives the family one balance sheet and a direct answer, and it gives me the full picture that the rest of the work depends on.

  • Start here

    Family office readiness assessment

    A fixed-fee engagement that consolidates everything the family owns onto one balance sheet, reviews how decisions are made, maps every fee, and answers whether the family should have a family office.

    How the assessment works

  • Governance and document review

    When the assessment's continuity test turns up gaps, I locate every trust agreement, operating agreement, and power of attorney, build a complete map of the family's entities, and work with the family's estate attorney to close each gap. Billed separately from the assessment.

  • Family office build-out

    For families the assessment says should build an office, I design the structure, recruit the staff, select the administrator, custodians, and reporting platform, and stay involved until the office runs on its own.
