About the name

William Faulkner took the title of his 1929 novel from that line, and I took the name of the firm from Faulkner. I studied accounting and English at the University of Mississippi in Oxford, his hometown.

The line also describes what many families experience: plenty of meetings, commentary, and reports from advisors who each see one piece, and very little clarity about the whole. The firm exists to supply that clarity.
