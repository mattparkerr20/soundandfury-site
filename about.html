Matt Parker

I have worked with family offices for seven years, inside a multi-family office, from a private bank, and as a consultant. I run every engagement myself.

I started as an equity derivatives trader at Deutsche Bank and BNP Paribas, and then joined J.P. Morgan's private bank in Houston. From 2013 to 2015 I was a Partner and Head of Business Development at Bienville Capital, a multi-family office in New Orleans. From 2017 to 2020 I returned to J.P. Morgan Private Bank in New Orleans as an Investment Specialist, covering family office clients and leading the Mississippi market.

In 2020 I founded Sound & Fury to consult for single family offices. For one, I built a global asset allocation and vetted direct investment opportunities. For the other, I worked across the family's entire balance sheet: a trust severance alongside its counsel, its alternatives program, and its marketable securities. From 2022 to 2026 I was a Partner and the Chief Compliance Officer of Search Fund Accelerator, an investment firm that backs entrepreneurs who acquire and run small companies.
